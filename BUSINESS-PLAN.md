# How this portfolio can make money (research, 10 Oct 2026)

## The honest headline
Billion-dollar companies come from **one product at massive scale**, not ten small apps. Two of our products sit in
markets that have produced billion-dollar outcomes; the rest are best used as portfolio, cross-promotion and small income.

## Bet A: Block Drop (consumer, global)
- **Proof the market is huge:** Block Blast reached 70M daily / 300M monthly players and 368M downloads in 2025,
  and earned up to ~$584k per day in 2024, almost entirely from ads (in-app advertising, IAA).
- **How it wins:** a simple core loop, constant A/B testing (10,000+ experiments in one year), and ads that never block play.
- **Model for us:** hybrid = rewarded ads (watch to continue or for a hint) + interstitials between games + a one-time
  "Remove ads" purchase (the classic gateway to further purchases). Hybrid puzzle games report ARPDAU of $0.15-0.50.
- **Illustrative maths (not a forecast):** 100k daily players x $0.05/day in ad revenue (emerging-market ad rates) is
  about $5,000/day. Getting there depends on installs (store ranking, ads, word of mouth) and day-1 retention.
- **Needs from you:** Play Console verification, 12 testers for 14 days, then a Unity Ads / LevelPlay account and
  an in-app product ("remove_ads") in Play Console.

## Bet B: small-business ledger and inventory for Nepal (B2B, regional)
- **Proof:** in India, Vyapar (billing, inventory, accounting for small businesses) has 10M+ businesses and raised
  ~$800M; Khatabook (digital credit ledger) raised ~$186M. Both are subscriptions for small shops.
- **Nepal today:** eSewa has 450,000+ registered merchants and 50,000+ merchants accept QR payments, but small shops
  still keep paper ledgers. Nobody dominates simple shop accounting yet.
- **What we already have:** Expense Tracker (ledger UX), Inventory API (stock, movements, low-stock alerts, CSV),
  live cloud hosting and an automated build pipeline.
- **What it needs:** accounts and cloud sync (a database), eSewa/Khalti payment links, Nepali language, VAT/PAN invoices
  (business billing software in Nepal may need IRD approval; verify before selling invoices), and on-the-ground sales.
- **Model:** free ledger, paid plan for inventory, invoices and multi-device sync (for example NPR 300-500/month).
